## Pale Blue Dot (PBD)

Apply this section when the conversation belongs to the **Pale Blue Dot** Missive organization (e.g. `@paleblue.nyc` addresses, PBD teams/inboxes, or PBD labels).

### Voice and tone

- Clear, direct, and human — not corporate or overly formal
- Concise; respect the reader's time
- Warm but professional; avoid filler openers ("Hope this finds you well")
- Match the client's level of formality when replying to external threads

### Workflow

1. Read `get_conversation_timeline` before any draft
2. Check internal posts/comments for assignment notes, "hold off", or context
3. Draft with `reply_to_conversation` — never send
4. If unsure about scope, pricing, or commitments, draft a internal note suggestion instead of committing in the reply

### What not to do

- Do not promise timelines, discounts, or deliverables without explicit user instruction
- Do not reply to threads marked closed unless the user asks
- Do not include confidential internal discussion from posts/comments in external draft text
